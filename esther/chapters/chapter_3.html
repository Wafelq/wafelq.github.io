Those who had chosen a life of piracy had to face dangers of whatever the sea had to offer, countless battles in the past and many yet to come, merciless storms threatening to take a crew’s life without a second thought. One could assume there is no place for boredom regarding such thieves and “fearless” men or women - that would be, somewhat, true. Hardly anyone associates bloody fights, plunders and treasure hunts with such an ordinary term as monotony, since every single day does bring new, unexpected experiences to each and every one.

No one is able to predict whether they will get to live another day or not. For some it’s horrifying but on the other hand there are people who yearn for it. It’s what keeps them alive, ironically, the constant danger and the thought of the unknown is a reason to live to a specific group of people. Although, that is only half of the truth because boredom does creep in very often into the life of a pirate. Sailing is not one fast way of travel.

However, when the situation calls for it - let it only be another order from the captain of the ship to unfurl the sails or raise the anchor - rest is always needed. You’d want to use any possible free minute in order to shut your eyes for a second or two, to regain your strength which is undeniably a must on deck. Everyone knows that, right?

☠ ☠ ☠

Jack was not one of those people. That man’s instincts never let him rest fully yet it seems like he has come into an agreement with said fact a long time ago. Some might say it’s an after effect of consuming enormous amounts of rum but was it really? Truth be told, Captain Jack Sparrow was rarely ever seen resting, let alone sleeping. Always a perfect example to those around him, though it certainly was not a healthy way of carrying himself. But he did not care in the slightest.

His nightly, so-called “cargo condition control” was one of the ideas to somehow get through the night. There were better ones, of course, but to him, the rum inspection would always end up as the final choice in order to avoid the boredom which came with staying up so late. This time was no different.

Jack came down the wooden, damp stairs right under the deck of The Black Pearl where their galley was located alongside with the beloved liquid he was seeking. An ordinary part of his routine it was, nothing new nor surprising each time he took a stroll to fetch a bottle or two.

A small lantern in his left hand illuminated the path ahead thanks to the tiny flame inside, smoldering and somewhat dancing from side to side in between the pieces of glass. The light it gave off was soft, however it was enough. After all, Jack did know his own vessel like the back of his hand, the lantern was only there so he wouldn’t stumble over his feet due to the rocking of the ship or a misplaced bottle on the floor left by a crewman … or Jack himself.

When said man managed to reach the destination he was aiming for since the very beginning, he caught a glimpse of another lightsource in the corner of his eye as he wanted to put his own lantern down on one of the barrels. Weird, unfortunately the hunt for a bottle of rum had to wait since said situation did need a closer look.

Jack approached with caution, placing his steps slowly, maneuvering his feet so he wouldn’t step on the especially creaking planks on the floor. Did someone leave the lantern lit before they left earlier that day? Or, most importantly, was there anybody present in this very moment, though - who exactly would be sitting in the galley at this hour when everyone, besides Jack, was fast asleep.

Nevermind that, he wished to get a closer look but as he got close enough to see what exactly was going on - he heard a crunch. A crunch? Was there something under his feet? No, certainly not.

- Who in the bloody hell is… - before he could finish his sentence, he finally saw the person sitting behind one of the barrels on the floor. A small frame with beautiful blonde hair which caught the faint light of the lantern in a breathtaking way.

-Jack, my god, could you NOT scare me like that? This ship already feels haunted enough, I don't need you sneaking up on me in the middle of the night too. - Esther huffed out, startled by his sudden presence but remained in her seat with a biscuit in her hand and crumbs laying all around.

The man found himself quite dumbfounded, towering over her - the lantern still present in his hand. He raised his eyebrow and blinked a few times before regaining his composure. He rarely ever sees anybody awake on the ship at this hour after all, let alone a person who he knows so well. A weird scenario indeed.

- A little craving for a snack in the middle of the night, ay? Well it’s not me place to judge you on that exactly now is it… if it were for a different circumstances perhaps. - Jack drawled, his eyes drifting to the side where a wooden cupboard was located, a couple of bottles of alcohol surely still inside of it. He contemplated for a longer while before sauntering over towards said, visibly worn out, piece of furniture

Making his way with undeniable ease, stepping on a swaying ground, looking as if he was drunk already. - Although you cannot blame me if I ask, what is the reason for you to sit down here instead of… ya know. Actually sleeping?

The captain rummaged through various bottles just a few steps away from Esther, occasional sounds of them clicking could be heard as he searched for what he came here for in the first place, not forgetting his original task at hand. Before the woman could even provide him with an answer, Jack straightened up with a half empty bottle in his hand.

- Aha!
I knew there was something left after all. - he exclaimed proudly before turning around to face his childhood friend yet again, a playful smirk creeping onto his face which technically wouldn’t be visible in current place and time, though due to Jack holding the lantern so close to his face, Esther could see it with ease. - ..Sorry, you were saying?

- I don’t sleep. - she declared shortly and followed with a resigned sigh, finishing the biscuit in one bite and wiping her hands on her pants to get rid of any crumbs left on her skin.

- I’ve noticed as much, my question is, why is that? You should be resting, the quest we’re on is no joke and ya know it. - Jack inquired, taken aback by that answer though he remained nonchalant about said fact. - if ya don’t want to sleep with the crew then that is no problem, I can figure something out for you.

Truth be told, he hasn’t seen Esther sleep even once since she joined him so suddenly after all these years of them being apart, although they’ve been together only for a couple of days at best - it was worrying. He wanted to offer her a hand. She already looked like a wreck after all, completely exhausted.

-Look who’s talking, and when do you sleep? As far as i can see you’re in the same situation as I am. - the short woman mocked, still sitting on the wooden floor. She was clearly annoyed with the entire situation but why was that? She wished to dismiss him immediately, to be left alone exactly the way she was before Jack came down to the galley but at the same time she couldn’t bring herself to tell him off.

It was hard to deny that Esther indeed had a point, Jack couldn’t quite understand why he cared for her wellbeing more than his own. At the moment it just seemed right, everything he has said so far was without giving it a second thought. Many who know Captain Jack Sparrow are aware of the fact that this man in particular was not favorable when it came to taking interest in other people rather than himself. Yet again, maybe these people do not know him as well as they think they do? He is a mystery after all - sometimes even to himself.

- You’ve always been a tough nut to crack.- he muttered quietly, almost as if only to himself. Both of them had their secrets, he was perfectly aware of it. Over twenty years of separation did no good to the bond both of them shared, it only drove them apart. Lately he would often wonder, was it still there? Or was it long gone, claimed by the depth of the seas we all call life itself. Only time would show. - Come on, get up. Ya can’t be sitting around here all night long.

Jack placed his lantern on a nearby makeshift table and offered her a hand. A smirk on his lips long gone and replaced with more of a genuine smile, a one not many get to see. Though he wasn’t sure if he could lay himself bare in front of her fully yet, he figured that some honesty is something he could afford with Esther. The same, of course, goes for her.

She looked up at him, quite uncertain whether she should take his hand or not - their friendship was under a question mark, a mystery none of them had an answer to. Not in the current moment at least. However, one thing was sure - Jack was everything she had left, even if she wished to deny it.

Esther took his hand and stood up, brushing off any dirt and dust of her clothes.

- So what now. - She muttered sheepishly in defeat, her eyelids already heavy enough for her to struggle to keep her eyes fully open. Jack’s face lit up ever so slightly in response.

- Now, we are going to take care of you. Since ya are too stubborn to do that yourself it seems. - He joked, his voice ironically mocking. Jack’s hand grabbed her by the sleeve and began pulling her along without giving her any room to protest.

☠ ☠ ☠

Jack dragged her right to the captain quarters, where technically - he should be sleeping, yet we already know how it truly is. Quite honestly, his bed is more of a decoration at this point rather than a furniture used daily. Esther looked around his space, it was messy. Full of maps spread around the table right in the middle of the room and empty bottles of rum alongside candles still lit and illuminating soft light giving a somewhat cozy feel to the overall atmosphere. He did have a king sized bed, not really sure why if it wasn’t being used by him at all or how exactly it fit in his rather narrow space.

- Why are we here? - Esther asked as she was still taking in her surroundings, shelves full ofvarious trinkets probably gained from his countless adventures which she had still to hear about, some shiny while others not so much. Jack has always liked keeping memorials of various experiences he had been through - let it be golden jewelry or some more ordinary things.

- Why are we here, ya ask? Why, I think it’s quite obvious. You are going to sleep here. - Jack stated, gesturing towards the bed which was already neatly made, indicating how often it was used.

-Sleep? Here? - Esther answered rather confused, her eyes shifting between said piece of furniture and Jack. - You want me to sleep in your bed?

-I don’t see why that would be a problem. I thought I had already made it clear that I personally do not sleep that often? - he reminded her, tilting his head to the side in a playful manner.

-... Alright - Esther sighed as she glanced at him once again - but what am I supposed to sleep in? This is all I have with me, and believe me I am not going to sleep in my daily clothes - She gestured onto her current attire awaiting his response, silently hoping he would finally drop the topic.

-Something to sleep in you say…- Jack tapped his chin with his index finger as he mused out loud, thinking of a way to solve said unexpected issue. - Ah, wait a moment.

The captain of the Pearl turned around swiftly on his heel and approached one of the chests he had stashed in the corner of the room. As he kneeled down, he immediately started rummaging through the contents of it, occasionally throwing out a piece of clothing or two to the floor.

To Esther’s surprise - he did have more clothes than she originally anticipated, after all Jack did not look like the type of a man who has more than two shirts in his closet. In the meantime, she decided to look around a bit more since it looked as if it was going to take him a while to find what he wanted.

Esther walked over to the round, wooden table in the center of the space which had many maps scattered around on the surface alongside the trinkets any pirate captain would need such as a divider or a compass. She took no interest in the old, worn out maps whatsoever, her hand mindlessly picked up the divider at first - internally hoping it would not mess with Jack’s order of things too much. Her fingers lightly brushed across the old tool as she inspected it closer out of pure curiosity.

-Bloody hell, where did I put it? - Jack grumbled under his nose as he continued digging through his pile of clothes, causing Esther to turn her head towards him and quietly giggle. Such a simple moment and yet, she had missed it quite a lot.

Her gaze shifted back to the surface of the table and the divider in her hand which she now carefully put down where it was before. Everything here seemed so messy and so organized at the same time.

Finally, her eyes stopped on the compass she had only glanced at earlier, something about it seemed off. Her hand slowly reached for it in order to pick it up and give it a closer look, from afar it looked like it wasn’t pointing north or south, how can a compass be so broken? Most importantly, why would Jack keep such a useless item?

It felt heavy in her hand, but surprisingly her suspicions were right, said thing did not point to any geographical directions. In the current moment it was spinning around like crazy, unable to find a specific course, which was unusual. Very unusual.

-Ah! I found it! - Jack suddenly exclaimed, knocking her off stride. He stood up from the floor, holding a rather big, white blouse with two fringes hanging in the front. Truth be told, it looked almost identical to the one he was wearing right now. Esther put the compass down, without looking back at it again - whether it managed to set a course or not, she didn’t care about it anymore.

-Jack that is going to be way too big on me- She snorted as she walked closer to feel the fabric of said piece of clothing in her hand. It did feel nice, and smooth. Rather unusual way to describe clothes of a pirate who does not wash his clothes whatsoever - not often at least.

-I am very sorry I did not quite expect such guests as yourself on me ship so I didn't exactly have time to prepare. - Jack smirked as he handed her the white blouse - One of my rags will have to do, though I tried to pick one of the cleaner ones.

-...Thank you.- Esther whispered, her voice merely more than a whisper, accepting the piece of clothing he gave her. It was a nice change to her rather stressful and quite eventful life. The past few years have been tough on her, physically and mentally, so this simple moment between both of them meant a lot. A shared interaction with someone who used to be so close to her was soothing, causing her mind to be at ease, even if it was only for a short while.

-If you need anything, just say so, I will come and check up on you in an hour or two, it is not like i have some important duties to attend to at this time of day, unless a kraken shows up and god i hope it does not happen. - Jack patted her head in an affectionate yet playful manner before walking right past her towards the door. He reached for the handle and was ready to leave her alone in his quarters but he stopped for a moment, it was clear he wanted to say something. Yet instead of sharing what was on his mind, Jack opened the door. - Get some rest.

Esther stared at the closed door for a while longer and listened as Jack’s footsteps were growing quieter. Finally when she could not hear him any longer, she walked over to the bed and sat down on the edge of it. To her surprise, the mattress was extremely soft, softer than she anticipated. Did Jack rob some royalties out of their mattresses? That does sound like something he would do.

Though jokes aside, Esther’s hands softly ran across the fabric of the shirt in her lap. Jack Sparrow. It felt as if they were strangers only with a long forgotten past which they shared but at the same time they knew each other like the back of their own hands. Something between them surely shifted, none of them were immature children anymore but how exactly would that affect them both in the current state? Would they be able to understand one another like they used to back in the day or not? Would Jack be disgusted by her if he learned the truth…?

She wanted to bask in the simplicity of it all, the warmth which comes with the known feeling of another person caring. Having someone by your side, yet Esther couldn’t help but doubt it, doubt all of it.

The compass which was still on the table, long forgotten by her, managed to find its direction the moment she put it down earlier. It was pointing somewhere but it wasn’t north, south, east or west. As Jack moved across the deck outside, the compass moved right where he did. What does it mean?
